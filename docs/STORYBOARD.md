# Babel and Abram (Genesis 11): storyboard

Ultra tier, 1080p60, every frame drawn in code. One idea carries the whole song: men try to make a
name for themselves in brick and fire, and God scatters it; then a name is given that no one built,
and it walks out of Ur on an open road. The first half lives in brick: every word the builders sing
is stamped into a brick and filled with black pitch, or fired into brick until it glows. God's words
are never in brick; they are light in cloud. After the scattering the words leave the brick: names in
river clay by small fires, ABRAM in living rock caught by the sunrise, and the last lines pressed into
the dust of a road beside real footprints. The running motif is the wind: one breath across one plain
at the start, the wind that scatters, and the wind on the open road at the end.

Places, few and built properly: the plain of Shinar (river silt, reed beds, a field of drying bricks,
kilns, the tower on the horizon), the tower close (its ramp, walls and terraces, its unfinished top in
the cloud), the river upstream at night, and the desert road west (sandstone ridges, the road, the well
and cairn at Haran). The tower is never shown as a clean block: close it is brick courses, pitch,
scaffold poles and cracked mortar; far it is a terraced mass in dust haze with fire lines climbing it.
No people are shown close; the only figures are a distant caravan against the dawn sky.
